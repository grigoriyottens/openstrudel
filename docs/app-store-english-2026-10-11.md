## App name

OpenStrudel

## Subtitle

Your everyday assistants

Your own assistants for writing, research and everyday tasks. Start on your Mac and pick up the conversation on iPhone or iPad.

## Keywords

assistant,writing,research,notes,files,tasks,planning,productivity,chat,personal

## Description

OpenStrudel is a free app for everyday tasks, writing and research. Create an editor, a researcher or a personal assistant. Give each one instructions and keep its conversations and files together.

Your assistants use language models to reply and work on tasks. They run on your own Mac or server. Connect your iPhone or iPad to continue the same conversations wherever you have access to that device.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

- Chat with your assistants and return to earlier conversations.
- Share photos and files, or dictate a message.
- Create assistants and change their instructions.
- Connect your services and schedule recurring tasks.
- Choose an available model and speed for all assistants or just one.
- Use assistants on several connected devices.

GETTING STARTED

Download the free Mac app from waiwai.is/openstrudel. Sign in to your own OpenAI account with access to Codex and create an assistant. In the Mac app's device settings, generate a connection link. Open it on your iPhone or iPad, or scan its QR code in the app, then confirm the connection.

You can also connect to your own server running OpenStrudel. You create the connection yourself. No company account, invitation from the developer or registration with OpenStrudel is required.

WHAT YOU NEED

Keep the Mac or server running and connected to the internet. On the same Wi-Fi network, your iPhone or iPad can connect directly to your Mac. Access from another network requires remote access to that Mac or server.

Model availability and usage limits depend on your separate OpenAI account. You do not need a separate OpenAI sign-in or API key on your iPhone or iPad. The app interface is currently in Russian.

YOUR DATA

Conversation history and files stay on the device where the assistant runs. With your permission, messages, selected files and relevant context are sent to OpenAI to generate replies. Other services are used only after you connect them.

FREE TO USE

OpenStrudel and all its features are free of charge. There are no subscriptions, in-app purchases or ads. Your model provider and any hosting provider you choose may charge separately for their services.

## Response to App Review

Thank you for reviewing OpenStrudel. It is a free consumer app for personal and work tasks. We request public App Store distribution. Anyone can download and set it up without approval from us. Build 34 replaces the rejected build 28.

Guideline 3.2 - Audience, access and payment

1. Is the app restricted to users of a single company or organization?

No. OpenStrudel does not require an employer, corporate email, contract or organization membership. The "employees" in the interface are software assistants powered by language models, not human employees of a company.

2. Is the app designed for a limited group of companies or organizations?

No. Any individual or organization can use it. There is no approved customer list, sales process or manual enrollment by WaiWai. The Mac app is publicly downloadable, free and without registration, at https://waiwai.is/openstrudel.

3. Which features are intended for the general public?

All features: conversations for planning, writing and research; attachments, dictation, assistant instructions and scheduled tasks. Users create assistants on their own Mac or server and use the same assistants from iPhone or iPad. The mobile app requires that connected device to be running. A connection link pairs the user's devices; it is not an invitation from us or approval to join an organization.

4. How do users obtain an account?

No WaiWai or OpenStrudel account is required. A user installs the free Mac app, signs in to their own OpenAI account with access to Codex, creates an assistant and generates a link or QR code to connect their iPhone or iPad. They can instead use their own configured server. No separate OpenAI sign-in is needed on the mobile device. The description and in-app help explain this setup.

For App Review only, our private connection page provides a running, isolated server with a configured OpenAI account and sample assistants. You do not need your own Mac, account, registration or payment. This is a review convenience, not a restricted customer signup process. The complete connection instructions are in App Review Information.

5. Is there any paid content, and who pays?

OpenStrudel and all its features are free of charge from WaiWai. We do not sell accounts, content, unlocks or subscriptions. Users may separately pay OpenAI and, if they choose their own server, a hosting provider. Those are external services, not payments to us. There is no purchase or checkout flow in the iPhone or iPad app. Review access is supplied free of charge.

Guideline 2.1 - Connection flow

We reproduced a failure when the review HTTPS page was pasted into the app; older builds accepted only the device link generated by that page. Build 34 recognizes HTTPS pages and opens the browser with a clearly labeled action. The page then opens OpenStrudel for device pairing. Invalid input displays an inline error. Direct device links still work.

The live review page, real model replies and chat persistence after relaunch passed on iPad Air 11-inch (M3), iPadOS 27.0, and iPhone 17, iOS 26.2, simulators. Eleven interface tests passed, including invalid, expired and reused links and connection recovery. Your exact input was not supplied, so we cannot confirm that it matched this reproduced case.

Guideline 4.1(c) - Metadata and icon

The subtitle is now "Your everyday assistants". The name, subtitle and keywords contain no third-party brands. All submitted descriptive text and review instructions are in English. The description accurately states that the app interface is currently in Russian and explains the separate OpenAI account required for normal use. Build 34 contains our current smiling pastry icon.
