# Build 45: Telegram reliability and reasoning preferences

Released from `64fa34f`, including PR #47 and the six earlier build-45 PRs.

## Changes

- Telegram no longer replies twice to `/start`. Owners can choose the employee
  who answers the bot's private chat. Voice messages queue instead of dropping;
  transcription is faster, ordinary videos are supported, and schedules created
  in Telegram deliver to their originating chat.
- Device abilities refresh after reconnection. The feed has less repeated
  information, and the assistant answers instead of displaying question cards.
- Device and employee reasoning preferences use the provider's native settings.
  Only reported levels are offered. Employees can inherit the device preference.
- The first owner client initializes the device time zone. New schedules and
  relative-date instructions use it; existing schedule time zones are preserved.
- New Telegram groups use contextual participation. Explicit existing settings
  are retained. Stale approval controls can be cleared across restarts without
  approving or replaying their actions.
- Home CPU and memory limits are configurable.
