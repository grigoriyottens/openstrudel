# OpenStrudel 1.0 (52)

Source: `c0fe203af4d0eecaca2a2aa3176bab65fb89b2e3`.

When multiple bots share a Telegram group, OpenStrudel now ignores commands
addressed to another bot and replies to another bot unless explicitly mentioned.
The recipes bridge pilot exposed the duplicate-answer bug; a failing routing
regression test reproduced it before the fix.

## Verified release

- All 12 Telegram test files passed: 99 tests. Typecheck passed.
- The packaged macOS runtime and installer checks passed: 5 tests.
- The app and DMG were signed with the existing Developer ID, notarized by
  Apple, stapled, and accepted by Gatekeeper.
- Downloads and stable aliases were published and independently downloaded
  over public HTTPS for hash verification. The signed Sparkle feed retains
  versions 51, 50 and 49; both feed and archive signatures were checked.
- The installed Mac updated from 51 to 52 through Sparkle's real UI. Its
  executable matches the staged release, its signature verifies, and the
  relaunched app shows personal, work and Grant devices online. The managed
  local rollback snapshot was marked healthy after these checks.
- All three Linux Homes use `openstrudel:52-c0fe203`. Each deployment waited
  for active work, kept a consistent rollback snapshot, checked SQLite, and
  verified live health and Telegram polling. Personal news collector timers
  were restored after quiescing writes. Conversation, message, employee and
  schedule counts did not decrease.
- A live command addressed to the new recipes bot received its answer without
  a second answer from the existing WaiClaw bot. This validates the original
  production overlap scenario; it does not claim every future group workflow.
